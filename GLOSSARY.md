# Tech Tavern

The public site of Tech Tavern, LLC: an IT and AI consultancy that publishes Articles to build credibility and presents its Consultants and their Services so prospective Clients can book a consultation.

## Language

### The business

**Tech Tavern**:
The consultancy itself (Tech Tavern, LLC), and the publisher of record for every Article.
_Avoid_: the blog, the site (when meaning the business)

**Client**:
An organization that engages Tech Tavern for paid work. The target Client is a Mission-Driven Organization.
_Avoid_: customer, user, visitor

**Mission-Driven Organization**:
A nonprofit, NGO, or public-sector body whose purpose is a mission rather than profit; Tech Tavern's intended audience and Client base.
_Avoid_: charity, NPO

### Publishing

**Article**:
A dated, published piece of writing by Tech Tavern. The one unit of editorial content on the site.
_Avoid_: post, blog post, entry

**Draft**:
An Article that exists but is withheld from every public surface: Article Index, Feed, sitemap, and its own Permalink.
_Avoid_: unpublished, hidden

**Publication Date**:
The calendar day an Article is published. Part of the Article's identity and its Permalink.
_Avoid_: created date, post date

**Last Modified**:
The calendar day an Article's content last materially changed. Equals the Publication Date until the Article is revised.
_Avoid_: updated, edited date

**Slug**:
The short, URL-safe name of an Article, unique within its Publication Date.
_Avoid_: handle, path

**Permalink**:
The permanent public address of an Article, formed from its Publication Date and Slug. Once published, it should never change.
_Avoid_: link, route

**Canonical URL**:
The single address search engines should treat as the authoritative copy of an Article. By default it is the Article's own Permalink; it is overridden only when the Article's original home is elsewhere.
_Avoid_: original link, source URL

**Excerpt**:
A short summary of an Article shown in listings and the Feed.
_Avoid_: summary, teaser, description

**Social Preview**:
The title, description, and image an Article presents when shared on social platforms; each part falls back to the Article's own title, Excerpt, and Featured Image.
_Avoid_: OG card, share card

**Featured Image**:
The lead image representing an Article in listings and Social Previews. A house default is used when none is chosen.
_Avoid_: hero image, thumbnail, cover

**Tag**:
A free-form topic label attached to an Article.
_Avoid_: category, keyword, label

**Reading Time**:
The estimated minutes needed to read an Article.
_Avoid_: read length

**Presentation**:
A downloadable slide deck that accompanies an Article.
_Avoid_: slides, deck

**Article Index**:
The newest-first, paged listing of all published Articles.
_Avoid_: archive, blog roll, articles list

**Feed**:
The RSS syndication of published Articles.
_Avoid_: RSS, newsletter

### Consulting

**Consultant**:
A person who delivers Tech Tavern's work and is presented publicly by a Consultant Profile. Articles are not bylined to Consultants.
_Avoid_: author, team member, expert

**Consultant Profile**:
The public page presenting one Consultant: headline, bio, Certifications, Services, Case Studies, Social Links, and Booking.
_Avoid_: bio page, about page, author profile

**Service**:
A specific, priced engagement a Consultant offers, with a price range and typical duration.
_Avoid_: offering, package, product

**Additional Service**:
A Service the Consultant offers outside their core AI-focused practice (e.g. general software or cloud work).
_Avoid_: secondary service, extra

**Service Area**:
One of Tech Tavern's broad capability pillars on the home page ("Tap into Insights / Impact / Solutions"). Not priced and not bookable.
_Avoid_: service (when meaning a pillar), practice area

**Case Study**:
A documented past engagement showing a Client outcome, published as a downloadable PDF.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, success story

**Certification**:
A professional credential a Consultant holds, shown with its badge and an optional verification link.
_Avoid_: badge, credential

**Booking**:
The way a prospective Client schedules a consultation with a Consultant, through a third-party Booking Provider.
_Avoid_: appointment, scheduling, contact form

**Booking Provider**:
The external scheduling service that hosts a Consultant's Booking calendar (Google, Calendly, or HubSpot).
_Avoid_: calendar, scheduler

**Social Link**:
A link from a Consultant Profile to the Consultant's account on a public social or code-hosting service.
_Avoid_: social, handle

**Vanity Redirect**:
A short, memorable address that forwards to a Consultant Profile (e.g. `/scott`).
_Avoid_: short link, alias
